# MerchantPay V0.4.1 — Customer Detail View

Added:
- Clicking a customer in the customer list opens a dedicated customer detail page.
- The page displays saved name, phone, email, notes, and customer creation date.
- Edit and Delete remain on the customer list.
- A Back to customers action returns to the list.
- The detail lookup is scoped to the authenticated merchant's business.
- An Activity section is reserved for future invoice/payment history.
