# MerchantPay V0.6 — Invoice Creation & Management

Implemented: invoice database models, customer/product ownership validation, automatic invoice numbering, draft creation, multiple lines, quantity/unit-price totals, due date, notes, invoice list/search, detail view, status management, Decimal-safe serialization, and direct invoice navigation.

Not included: payment processing, payment links, reminders, PDF generation, taxes/discounts, and post-creation line editing.
