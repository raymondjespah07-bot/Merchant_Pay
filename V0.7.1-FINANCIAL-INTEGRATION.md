# MerchantPay V0.7.1 — Financial Integration

Built from the working V0.7 baseline.

## Added
- Dashboard financial metrics backed by real completed Payment records.
- Collected, awaiting, overdue, today, month and total invoiced figures.
- Dashboard recent-payments table with direct invoice links.
- Payments page redesigned as an organized payment-history table.
- Customer details now include total invoiced, total paid and outstanding balance.
- Customer payment history table with invoice links, methods and references.
- Customer invoice history with balance per invoice.
- Business ownership remains enforced through `currentBusiness()` and scoped database queries.

## Design principle
Payments remain record-keeping only. MerchantPay does not process or move customer money.

## Note
Outstanding dashboard totals are calculated from invoice totals minus completed payments. Overdue is based on invoice due dates for invoices not paid or cancelled.
